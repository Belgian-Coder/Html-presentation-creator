# A 20-minute test with a real first-time user

The editor was checked by simulated first-time users. This sheet is for checking it with a real person who has never seen it, without a manual.

## Before
- **The person:** someone who has made slides in PowerPoint or Google Slides, but has never seen this editor.
- **The file:** a copy of `Presentation.html`, opened in Chrome or Edge. They can press **E** or the pencil button at the bottom to edit.
- **What to say:** "Think aloud. If you are stuck for a minute, tell me and we go on. You can't break anything."
- **What not to do:** don't help and don't point. Only note what happens.

## The tasks (read one at a time)
1. Add a new slide with a title and three bullet points. Make one point stand out.
2. Put a picture on the slide, and use a picture of your own.
3. Make the title fly in when you click, and look at how it plays.
4. Change the colours of the whole presentation.
5. Write what you want to say on this slide (speaker notes).
6. Show the slide number on every slide.
7. Centre a text, then turn a list into a numbered list.
8. Undo your last three changes, and see what was undone.
9. Draw on a slide while presenting.
10. Add background music to a slide.
11. Reuse slides from another presentation file.
12. Print a handout with your notes.
13. Move a part of a slide somewhere else, then put the slide back as it was.
14. Save your work.
15. Find the keyboard shortcut for copying.
16. Make a slide that only shows when you ask for it (for questions).
17. Check the spelling of all slides.

## Note per task
| Task | Where they looked first | Done? (yes / with trouble / no) | Time | What they said or where they hesitated |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## After
- **Ask:** "What was hardest?" and "What did you expect to find somewhere and not find?"
- **Fix:** every "no", and every "with trouble" that two people have.
  - The place they looked first is where the feature should be, or point to.
  - The words they used are what the label or the Ctrl+K search should understand.
- **Record:** the results below. The simulated checks got 15 of 17 done; the other 2 were limited by the test's file chooser (v198).
